# Dot Muncher 🐍

A gentle 3D snake game made for a little one — calm, colorful, and impossible to lose.

**Play:** https://staymunch.github.io/dot-muncher/

## How to play
- **Drag anywhere** to steer the snake — it glides smoothly wherever your finger leads.
- **Eat the glowing dots** to grow longer. Each one sparkles and plays a soft chime.
- **Friendly snakes** roam the endless field too, munching dots alongside you.

## Made gentle on purpose
- **No "game over."** Nothing can hurt you — the other snakes are harmless, there are no walls, and the field never ends.
- **Slow, fluid movement** that's easy for young eyes to follow.
- **Calm visuals** — soft sky, gentle colors, no flashing.
- **Sound is soft and mutable** — tap the speaker in the corner to silence it.

## On an iPhone / iPad
Open the link in Safari, then **Share → Add to Home Screen** to play full-screen like a real app (works offline once loaded).

## Tech
A single self-contained `index.html` — a small custom **WebGL** renderer (glossy lit spheres, soft shadows, fog), no libraries, no build step, no internet needed after first load.
